Electricity markets & power systems

Distributed markets.
Secure power systems.

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at Southeast University, China. My research focuses on distributed electricity markets and secure power system operation.

I am particularly interested in decentralized market coordination, mechanism design, and the integration of distributed energy resources. I develop optimization and learning methods that connect market decisions with physical network constraints.

My work spans peer-to-peer energy trading, distributed optimization, and multi-agent reinforcement learning. I am also exploring diffusion models for characterizing feasible operating regions and supporting secure decision-making in power systems.
